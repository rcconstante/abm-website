# Your college preparation checklist

An original planning aid for students exploring business-related college programs. Requirements, fees, available support, and deadlines vary; verify them directly with each institution.

## Explore your interests

- [ ] List three subjects or activities you enjoy and explain why.
- [ ] Try the ABM career quiz as a reflection exercise, not an aptitude test.
- [ ] Compare accountancy, management, marketing, finance, human resources, and entrepreneurship.
- [ ] Discuss your interests and constraints with a parent, guardian, teacher, or guidance counselor.

## Prepare your application

- [ ] Read the current official admission instructions.
- [ ] Prepare only the documents the institution requests.
- [ ] Ask how transcripts and school records must be submitted.
- [ ] Use a personal email address you can access reliably.
- [ ] Save deadlines in a calendar with reminders.
- [ ] Keep copies of submitted forms and reference numbers securely.
- [ ] Check scholarship conditions and renewal requirements.
- [ ] Confirm any fees through the institution's official channels.
- [ ] Avoid sending identification documents to unofficial accounts.

## Ask better questions

- What topics will I actually study in the first year?
- Are there requirements linked to my SHS curriculum or subject choices?
- What support exists if I need extra help in mathematics or writing?
- What practical experiences are available to students?
- Does my intended profession require licensure, certification, or further study?
- What is the total expected cost beyond tuition?
