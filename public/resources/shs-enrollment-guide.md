# Senior High School (ABM) Enrollment & Requirements Guide

**Westbridge Senior High — Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM) Strand**  
*Demo admissions guide for incoming Grade 11 learners and parents/guardians.*

---

## 1. Who Can Enroll in ABM?

Incoming Grade 11 students who have completed Junior High School (Grade 10) and are interested in business, accounting, finance, marketing, management, human resources, or entrepreneurship. No prior accounting or business background is required.

## 2. Step-by-Step Enrollment Process

1. **Explore & Inquire** — Review the ABM subjects and career pathways, or submit an inquiry / campus tour request through the Contact page.
2. **Prepare Your Documents** — Gather your Grade 10 academic records and identification documents listed below.
3. **Strand Advising & Assessment** — Attend a short advising session with the ABM Strand Coordinator or Guidance Office to review your goals and schedule.
4. **Submit Requirements & Confirm Slot** — Submit verified copies at the Admissions Office (Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM) to receive your official class section and orientation schedule.

## 3. Documentary Requirements Checklist

- [ ] Original & photocopy of Grade 10 Report Card (SF9 / Form 138)
- [ ] Photocopy of PSA Birth Certificate
- [ ] Certificate of Good Moral Character from Junior High School
- [ ] Junior High School Completion Certificate (or Certification of Candidacy for Graduation)
- [ ] Two (2) recent 2×2 ID photos with white background
- [ ] DepEd SHS Voucher Certificate / ESC Grantee Certificate (if applicable)
- [ ] Completed Student & Parent/Guardian Information Sheet

## 4. Key Enrollment Milestones (SY 2027–2028 Sample Calendar)

## 5. ABM Strand Quick Snapshot

- **Specialized Subjects:** Fundamentals of ABM 1 & 2, Organization & Management, Principles of Marketing, Business Finance, Business Mathematics, Applied Economics, Business Ethics & Social Responsibility, Business Enterprise Simulation.
- **Applied Subjects:** Entrepreneurship, Practical Research, Empowerment Technologies, English for Academic & Professional Purposes.
- **Core Subjects:** Oral Communication, Reading & Writing, General Mathematics, Statistics & Probability, and other SHS Core subjects.
