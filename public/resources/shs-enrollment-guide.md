# Senior High School (ABM) Enrollment & Requirements Guide

**Our Lady of Lourdes College Foundation (OLLCF) — Senior High School Department**  
**Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM) Strand · Daet, Camarines Norte**  
*"Here at OLLCF, Your Future is our Concern." — Quality · Excellence · Success*

---

## 1. Who Can Enroll in ABM at OLLCF?

Incoming Grade 11 students (New, Transferee, Continuing, or Returnee) who have completed Junior High School (Grade 10) and want to build strong foundations in accounting, business administration, finance, marketing, management, human resources, or entrepreneurship. No prior accounting or business background is required.

### Why Choose OLLCF Senior High School?
- Academic & Tech-Pro tracks designed to prepare learners for college and future careers
- Quality Lourdenian education and a supportive campus community in Daet, Camarines Norte
- **Free school supplies & uniform** for Senior High School enrollees *(confirm latest promo guidelines with Admissions)*
- Active student leadership and hands-on enterprise opportunities through the **Association of Young Business Leaders (AYBL)**, including campus events like the **OLLCF SHS Food Bazaar**

---

## 2. Documentary Requirements Checklist

Ready to enroll at OLLCF Senior High School? Prepare the following documents:

- [ ] **Grade 10 Report Card (Original)** (SF9 / Form 138)
- [ ] **Certificate of Good Moral Character** from Junior High School
- [ ] **PSA Birth Certificate** (Photocopy)
- [ ] **2 pcs 2×2 ID Picture** (with nametag, white background)
- [ ] **DepEd SHS Voucher Certificate / ESC Grantee Certificate** *(if applicable)*

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Enrollment Process

1. **Explore the ABM Strand** — Review ABM subjects, career pathways, and take the 2-minute Career Match Quiz on this site.
2. **Prepare Your Documents** — Gather the original report card, good moral certificate, PSA copy, and 2×2 nametag ID photos listed above.
3. **Visit OLLCF Admissions or Pre-Register** — Head to the Our Lady of Lourdes College Foundation campus along Vinzons Avenue, Daet, Camarines Norte, or check the official OLLCF and OLLCF SHS Facebook pages for pre-registration announcements.
4. **Confirm Section & Join AYBL** — Once enrolled, check OLLCF SHS official announcements for section assignments, welcome orientation, and how to get involved with the Association of Young Business Leaders (AYBL).

## 4. ABM Strand Learning Areas Snapshot

- **Specialized Subjects:** Fundamentals of ABM 1 & 2, Organization & Management, Principles of Marketing, Business Finance, Business Mathematics, Applied Economics, Business Ethics & Social Responsibility, Business Enterprise Simulation.
- **Applied Subjects:** Entrepreneurship, Practical Research, Empowerment Technologies, English for Academic & Professional Purposes.
- **Core Subjects:** Oral Communication, Reading & Writing, General Mathematics, Statistics & Probability, and other SHS Core subjects.
