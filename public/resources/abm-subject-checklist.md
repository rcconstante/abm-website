## Accounting foundations

- [ ] Explain assets, liabilities, and owner's equity.
- [ ] Show how a transaction affects the accounting equation.
- [ ] Describe what a journal, ledger, and trial balance do.
- [ ] Distinguish an income statement from a statement of financial position.
- [ ] Explain why profit and cash flow are different.

## Business mathematics & finance

- [ ] Calculate a percentage increase and decrease.
- [ ] Explain the difference between markup and margin.
- [ ] Compare simple and compound interest in a classroom exercise.
- [ ] Prepare a simple budget with clear assumptions.
- [ ] Identify risks and limitations in a financial comparison.

## Management & teamwork

- [ ] Turn a project goal into tasks, owners, and deadlines.
- [ ] Explain planning, organizing, leading, and controlling.
- [ ] Listen to feedback and revise a team plan.
- [ ] Record decisions and communicate changes clearly.

## Marketing & entrepreneurship

- [ ] Describe a customer need using evidence.
- [ ] Identify a customer segment without stereotyping.
- [ ] Explain product, price, place, and promotion.
- [ ] Compare a business idea with current alternatives.
- [ ] Create a short, truthful product pitch.

## Economics, ethics & research

- [ ] Explain a basic supply-and-demand scenario.
- [ ] Identify stakeholders affected by a business decision.
- [ ] Consider privacy, fair treatment, and environmental impact.
- [ ] Ask permission before collecting research responses.
- [ ] Distinguish a factual claim from an unsupported opinion.
- [ ] Cite the sources used in a presentation or paper.

## Communication & college preparation

- [ ] Deliver a short presentation with a clear structure.
- [ ] Write a respectful inquiry with a specific question.
- [ ] Compare at least three college programs and their requirements.
- [ ] Ask my school which curriculum and subjects apply to my cohort.
